# Sample characters

Populated by `npm run assets` (also runs on `npm install`). See `manifest.json` for the
list, licenses and sources. All models are free to use; check each model's embedded
VRM meta / source page before redistributing.

Drop your own `.vrm`, `.glb`, `.gltf` or `.fbx` characters in here (and add them to
`manifest.json`) or simply drag them onto the studio window at runtime.

## Where to find more rigged characters (incl. animals)

- https://opensourceavatars.com / https://github.com/ToxSam/open-source-avatars - 300+ CC0
  VRM avatars (Polygonal Mind "100 Avatars" R1-R3: bears, tiger, wolf, fox, rabbit,
  penguin, shark, cow, pig, frog, cat, dinosaur, ... plus stylised adult men). Direct
  `.vrm` links; the animals and the adult male avatars bundled here come from this registry.
- https://github.com/hinzka/52blendshapes-for-VRoid-face - male and female VRoid bodies
  with all 52 ARKit blendshapes (blink, gaze, jaw, visemes). The Perfect Sync characters
  bundled here come from that release. Credit hinzka and the VRoid sample terms.
- https://hub.vroid.com - search "kemono" / "furry" / "bear"; check each model's
  download + use permissions (many are view-only).
- https://booth.pm - free VRM/VRChat "kemono" avatars (e.g. Kumagurumi bear by Usaneko,
  UV license, no redistribution). Load the `.vrm` from the zip via Import.
- https://www.mixamo.com - humanoid FBX characters (Mousey, Goblin, Mutant, Ty...). Needs
  a free Adobe login; export "T-pose", FBX binary, then import or convert to GLB.
- https://readyplayer.me - human avatars as GLB with ARKit blendshapes.

Any bipedal rig with a humanoid bone layout works. Quadrupeds do not - the VMC stream is
a human skeleton.
